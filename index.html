Selected Publications

2025

  1. Elsevier
    Spatiotemporal Air Quality Prediction in the United States Using Machine Learning and Deep Learning Models
    Md. Bipul Islam, Mandal, Ashis Kumar, Roy, Dino Nath, and Nowroj, Farhan
    Not published yet, will publish soon in Environmental Modelling & Software
    Abs Bib

    This study presents a comprehensive machine learning and deep learning approach for spatiotemporal air quality prediction across the United States. We develop advanced predictive models that integrate meteorological data, geographical features, and historical pollution measurements to forecast air quality indices with high accuracy. The framework employs ensemble methods combining traditional machine learning algorithms with state-of-the-art deep learning architectures including LSTM networks and attention mechanisms to capture complex temporal dependencies and spatial correlations. Our models demonstrate superior performance in predicting PM2.5, PM10, ozone, and other critical air pollutants across diverse geographical regions and seasonal patterns. The results provide valuable insights for environmental policy making and public health protection strategies.

  2. Springer
    From Income to Intake: A Data Science Approach to Understanding Socioeconomic Influences on Nutritional Choices in U.S. Households
    Md. Bipul Islam, Rakib, Md. Rakib, Islam, Md. Rafiul, and Hossain, Md. Mintu
    Not published yet, will publish soon in Journal of Nutrition & Health
    Abs Bib

    This research examines the complex relationship between socioeconomic factors and nutritional choices in U.S. households using advanced data science methodologies. We analyze large-scale datasets to understand how income levels, education, geographic location, and demographic characteristics influence dietary patterns and food consumption behaviors. Our study employs machine learning techniques including clustering analysis, predictive modeling, and feature importance analysis to identify key socioeconomic determinants of nutritional intake. The findings reveal significant disparities in food access and quality across different income groups, providing crucial insights for public health policy and nutrition intervention programs. This work contributes to understanding food insecurity and nutritional inequality in American society.

  3. ACM
    From Local to Global: Comparative Forecasting of Internet Adoption Across Countries Using Machine Learning Approaches
    Md. Bipul Islam, Rakib, Md. Abdur, Tareq, Matiur Rahman, and Mandal, Ashis Kumar
    Not published yet, will publish soon in ACM Transactions on Internet Technology
    Abs Bib

    This paper presents a comprehensive comparative analysis of internet adoption patterns across different countries using machine learning approaches. We develop predictive models that forecast internet penetration rates by analyzing economic indicators, infrastructure development, demographic factors, and technological readiness indices. Our methodology employs various machine learning algorithms including time series forecasting, regression analysis, and ensemble methods to predict internet adoption trends from local to global scales. The study reveals significant variations in adoption patterns between developed and developing nations, identifying key factors that accelerate or hinder internet penetration. The results provide valuable insights for policymakers and technology companies planning digital infrastructure investments and connectivity initiatives worldwide.

  4. Wiley
    An Explainable AI Framework for Understanding Psychosocial and Demographic Factors in U.S. Drug Addiction Prediction
    Md. Bipul Islam, Roy, Dino Nath, Islam, Md. Rafiul, and Hossain, Md. Mintu
    Not published yet, will publish soon in Expert Systems with Applications
    Abs Bib

    This study introduces an explainable artificial intelligence framework for understanding and predicting drug addiction in the United States based on psychosocial and demographic factors. We develop interpretable machine learning models that can identify key risk factors and provide transparent explanations for addiction predictions. Our framework integrates various data sources including social determinants of health, mental health indicators, economic conditions, and demographic characteristics to build comprehensive predictive models. The explainable AI approach ensures that healthcare professionals can understand the reasoning behind predictions, making the system suitable for clinical decision support. The study contributes to addiction prevention strategies and personalized intervention programs by providing actionable insights into the complex factors contributing to substance abuse.

  5. IEEE
    Leveraging Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Large Language Models to Predict Healthcare Utilization and Costs in the United States
    Md. Bipul Islam, Rakib, Md. Abdur, Nowroj, Farhan, and Islam, Md. Rakib
    Not published yet, will publish soon in IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics
    Abs Bib

    This research leverages machine learning, deep learning, and large language models to predict healthcare utilization patterns and associated costs in the United States. We develop advanced predictive models that analyze patient demographics, medical history, treatment patterns, and healthcare system characteristics to forecast future healthcare needs and expenditures. Our approach combines traditional machine learning algorithms with cutting-edge deep learning architectures and incorporates natural language processing of medical records using large language models. The study addresses critical challenges in healthcare resource allocation, cost prediction, and patient care optimization. The results provide valuable insights for healthcare administrators, policymakers, and insurance providers to improve healthcare delivery efficiency and cost management strategies.
